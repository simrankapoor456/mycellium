# Phase 9A.2 Storyboard

## Reference policy

Five uploaded images informed the implementation. They were private compositional references only and are not repository assets, production frames, screenshots, social assets, or browser requests.

The implementation does not reproduce the images pixel for pixel, trace them, crossfade them, or animate them as an image sequence. The production scene is original SVG and DOM geometry using Mycellium's forest-black, mineral, antique-gold, and intelligence-lime tokens.

## How the references informed the shipped result

| Reference | Cue retained | Shipped interpretation |

The later canopy, blossoms, fruit, product-value labels, and renewal seed were designed from Mycellium's product model, not copied from the references.

## Actual storyboard

1. **Seed of intent:** one warm seed rests at the soil horizon.
2. **First root:** the shell opens; the crack and radicle appear; one primary root draws to its full depth.
3. **Branching roots:** child paths spread from the primary root, connections follow, and the seven-area Foundation becomes legible.
4. **Trunk:** an architecture spine draws upward without replacing the evidence below.
5. **Branches:** accountable product boundaries divide from the shared spine.
6. **Leaves:** stories and tasks grow at branch endpoints.
7. **Blossoms:** review checkpoints appear where judgment remains necessary.
8. **Fruit:** grounded context, reviewable structure, and portable Blueprint value become tangible.
9. **Mature product value:** labels make the three value outcomes explicit while lineage remains visible.
10. **New seed:** learning follows a new dotted path and becomes renewed intent.

## Continuity rules

Every beat inherits the geometry and meaning of the beat before it:

1. The radicle begins inside the seed.
2. The primary root begins at the radicle.
3. Every Foundation root begins on the primary root.
4. Cross-connections appear only after their endpoint branches.
5. The trunk begins at the same seed that anchors the roots.
6. Every canopy branch begins on the trunk.
7. Leaves, blossoms, and fruit stay attached to the same canopy.
8. Mature-value labels point to existing fruit rather than replacing the organism.
9. The renewal path begins at mature value and carries a new seed outward.
10. Reverse scrolling removes these changes in the exact inverse order.

This continuity prevents the experience from reading as ten slides.

## Product and visual restraint

The composition remains technical and organic:

- root states preserve uncertainty instead of turning the organism uniformly green
- lime indicates meaningful activation and confirmed structure
- antique gold marks the seed, fruit lineage, and renewal
- blossoms are small review markers, not decorative celebration
- all geometry is fixed and deterministic
- no particles, bounce, elastic motion, parallax field, idle drift, or autoplay loop is present

The final state is not a generic mature tree. It is a living product system whose evidence, structure, work, review, output, and learning remain visibly connected.

## Responsive storyboard

Desktop and tablet preserve one reversible scroll-linked composition beside semantic chapters. Mobile keeps the visual close to the reading position and interpolates only when a chapter becomes dominant. Reduced motion presents the full ten-stage outcome at once, with compact natural-flow copy beneath it.

## Continuation

The storyboard is complete at the new seed. A later Phase 9B may connect renewed context to actual product lineage or version history. It should start from this seed and preserve the established geometry rather than adding more decorative plant stages.
